# Privacy Policy — Swipe to Skip for umingle

_Last updated: October 4, 2026 (v1.2.0)_

Swipe to Skip uses your webcam to detect a right-swipe hand gesture and press the skip button on umingle.com.

**Camera.** Video from your camera is processed entirely on your device, inside the extension, using Google's MediaPipe hand-tracking, face-landmark, and selfie-segmentation models. No images, video, hand, or face data are recorded, stored, or sent anywhere by the extension.

**Camera filters.** If you turn on a filter, the extension replaces the video umingle sends with the filtered version. umingle transmits that video to the person you're chatting with, exactly as it would your normal camera. The extension itself sends nothing.

**The other person's video.** If you turn on the jumpscare, the extension looks at small snapshots of the other person's video, on your device only, to notice when they show a hand. Snapshots are discarded immediately and never stored or sent anywhere.

**Jumpscare.** When it triggers, the extension briefly replaces your outgoing video with the scare image and mixes a scream into your outgoing microphone audio; umingle sends that to the other person like your normal camera and mic.

**Stored settings.** The extension saves only its own settings in your browser's local extension storage: on/off state, sensitivity, panel position, whether the preview is collapsed, your filter choices, jumpscare settings (including any image or sound you chose), and the skip button you picked. These never leave your browser.

**Network.** If the model files are not bundled with the extension, they are downloaded from Google's public model storage (`storage.googleapis.com`) when first needed. These requests carry no personal data. The extension makes no other network requests and contains no analytics or tracking.

**Third parties.** No data is sold or shared with anyone.

**Contact.** Open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
