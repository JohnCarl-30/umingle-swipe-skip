# Privacy Policy — Swipe to Skip for umingle

_Last updated: October 4, 2026_

Swipe to Skip uses your webcam to detect a right-swipe hand gesture and press the skip button on umingle.com.

**Camera.** Video from your camera is processed entirely on your device, inside the extension, using Google's MediaPipe hand-tracking model. No images, video, or hand data are recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.

**Stored settings.** The extension saves only its own settings in your browser's local extension storage: on/off state, sensitivity, panel position, whether the preview is collapsed, and the skip button you picked. These never leave your browser.

**Network.** If the hand-tracking model file is not bundled with the extension, it is downloaded once from Google's public model storage (`storage.googleapis.com`). This request carries no personal data. The extension makes no other network requests and contains no analytics or tracking.

**Third parties.** No data is sold or shared with anyone.

**Contact.** Open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
